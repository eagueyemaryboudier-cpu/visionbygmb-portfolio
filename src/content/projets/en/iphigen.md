## The question

Diesel and grid power cost SMEs in Côte d'Ivoire more than USD 0.35/kWh. Iphigen offers energy at around USD 0.15/kWh, with financing matched to cash flow. The website had to make this difference concrete for the manager of a hotel, a clinic or an agri-food business.

## Business plan data

The figures shown on the site (price per Wp, cost per kWh, savings rate, payback period) come from the client's business plan. They feed the simulator directly.

## A simulator instead of a sales pitch

Visitors drag their monthly energy bill, from 200,000 to 6 million FCFA. The site instantly shows the saving per month, per year and over ten years, along with the CO₂ avoided. The calculation uses the savings rate from Iphigen's price list, and the precise quote still follows an audit.

## From identity to launch

I handled the whole project: logo, mock-ups, copy, Express.js development, French, English and Spanish versions, audit request form and launch. The site was delivered in three months. It is now administered by the client.
